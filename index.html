Meet Our Coaches

Experienced coaches. Safe surfing. Confident surfers. Whether you're a complete beginner, a young surfer developing their skills, or an experienced surfer looking to improve, our coaches provide practical guidance and encouragement every step of the way.

Dave Jennings

Founder | Qualified Surf Coach (SSA) | Competitive Surfer

With more than 48 years of surfing experience, 37 years in the surf industry and over 30 years of coaching, Dave brings exceptional depth of knowledge to every lesson — focused on building confidence, good technique, and respect for the ocean.

  • Natal Shortboard & Longboard Champion
  • Represented KwaZulu-Natal at two SA Longboarding Championships
  • 2nd place at two South African Championships
  • 3 years' experience in adaptive surf coaching
  • South African Lifesaving Award
For Parents: Dave's extensive surfing, coaching and lifesaving experience means students learn not only how to surf, but how to develop good ocean awareness and safe habits.

Clive Greenstone

Qualified Surf Coach (SSA) | Sports Coach | Adaptive Surf Volunteer

Clive brings more than 40 years of surfing experience and five years of sports coaching to the school. His time as a competitive surfer, coach and volunteer has shaped a patient, encouraging teaching style.

  • KwaZulu-Natal Longboarding competitor
  • Represented KZN at the SA Bodyboarding Championships
  • Volunteer adaptive surf coach
  • South African Lifesaving Award holder
  • Level 2 First Aid qualified
For Parents: Clive understands children have different levels of confidence in the ocean. He helps each student progress at their own pace.
